Two laws that keep it honest and safe

confirmed = vuln reproduces & fix stops it

A finding counts only when the vulnerable rebuild is exploited and the hardened rebuild refuses the same exploit. That pairing kills the false positive that “reproduces” no matter what — the disease every scanner suffers.

rebuild, don’t attack

Phoenix hosts and hacks a reproduction it built, never your running service. Recreating a pattern to prove it is defensive research; aiming it at live infrastructure is a gated, human-only act — and the cage removes the capability, so it can’t happen by accident.
